Without it: bare hands with the Combat System mod, else the host's weapons. Damage only counts inside the fight area (a map's area, or one the host drags or draws in the lobby). At 0 health you're knocked down for 10 s - hit again and you're out; last team standing wins, or the most health left when time runs out.
